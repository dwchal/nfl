About the data & prediction model

The schedule and scores come from nflverse’s public game data. Saved data refreshes after one hour; use Refresh data for an immediate update. The app shows the latest available season and supports earlier seasons. Preseason games are excluded.

Each team starts at 1,500 Elo. Ratings use up to three preceding seasons, carry two-thirds of their distance from 1,500 into each new season, and update after each completed game in date order. The update factor is 20. Home teams receive a 55-point advantage for the expected result; neutral venues receive none. Actual ties count as half a win for Elo and winning percentage.

Upcoming matchup probabilities use today’s ratings within the selected season. The outlook simulates all remaining regular-season games 10,000 times, updating ratings along each simulated path. The middle 80% range describes the simulation’s outcomes, not a calibrated confidence interval. Future ties are not simulated. The forecast predicts regular-season wins; it does not predict playoff qualification.

This is a simple, uncalibrated model. It does not account for injuries, roster changes, rest, weather, scoring margin, or betting markets. Historical season views use the final available results for that season; they are not forecasts made at a historical date.
